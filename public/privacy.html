Cyclix Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 5, 2025

Your Privacy Matters: Cyclix is committed to protecting your personal health information. We use industry-standard encryption and never sell your data to third parties.

1. Data We Collect

To provide our personalized health tracking services, we collect:

  • Menstrual Health Data: Cycle dates, flow intensity, symptoms, and mood tracking
  • Pregnancy Information: Due dates, pregnancy symptoms, and related health metrics
  • Account Information: Email address and encrypted authentication credentials
  • Health Integration: Optional data from Apple Health/Google Fit (with your explicit consent)
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous app usage patterns to improve our services

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information enables us to:

  • Generate accurate cycle predictions and fertility window calculations
  • Provide personalized health insights and recommendations
  • Send relevant notifications and reminders based on your preferences
  • Improve app functionality through anonymous analytics
  • Deliver educational content tailored to your health journey
  • Detect irregular patterns that may require medical attention

3. Data Protection Measures

  • Encryption: All health data encrypted using AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit
  • Biometric Security: Optional biometric authentication for app access
  • Secure Infrastructure: Data stored on Google Cloud Platform with enterprise-grade security
  • Regular Audits: Quarterly security assessments and penetration testing
  • Data Minimization: We only collect data necessary for app functionality
  • No Third-Party Sales: We never sell your personal health data to advertisers or data brokers

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We only share data with:

  • Firebase/Google Cloud: For secure data storage and app functionality
  • Health Platforms: Apple Health/Google Fit (only with your explicit consent)
  • Research Partners: Anonymous, aggregated data for women's health research (opt-in only)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect user safety

5. Your Rights & Controls

  • Data Export: Download all your data via Settings > Export Data
  • Account Deletion: Permanently delete your account and all associated data
  • Consent Management: Modify your privacy preferences at any time
  • Data Access: Request a copy of all personal data we have about you
  • Data Correction: Update or correct any inaccurate information
  • Opt-Out: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

6. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your residence. We ensure adequate protection through:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for EU data transfers
  • Adequacy decisions where applicable
  • Additional safeguards for sensitive health data

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. After account deletion, data is permanently removed within 30 days, except where required by law.

8. Children's Privacy

Cyclix is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users aged 13-17 require parental consent in certain jurisdictions.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We'll notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification.
